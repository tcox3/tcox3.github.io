Russian Aerial Attacks Against Ukraine

This original dataset records Russian missile and UAV attacks against Ukraine using official Ukrainian Air Force reports published on Telegram. It includes 928 attack events that document the number of weapons launched and intercepted alongside launch, target, and interception locations. Each weapon observation is linked to its Telegram source message, providing a transparent and reproducible record for the study of aerial warfare and air-defense. The dataset supports the analysis in Strength in Numbers? Assessing the Effectiveness of Large-Scale Drone Attacks in the Russia-Ukraine War.
